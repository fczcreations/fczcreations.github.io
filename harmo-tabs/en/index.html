How it works

  1. Open a MIDI or KAR file

    The app finds the melody track on its own, or the vocal line in a karaoke file.

  2. Choose your harmonica

    Diatonic or chromatic, automatic key or your own, lower or higher octave.

  3. Save the tab

    As a PDF to print, or as an Excel file to edit.

How to read a Harmo Tabs tab

Each line is split by a horizontal rule: draw notes are above it, blow notes below. The number is the hole.

4e!4d2564
4Hole 4, natural note
0Hole 10
eOne bar: half-step bend (hole 3 here)
dTwo bars: whole-step bend
cThree bars: step-and-a-half bend
TSmall circle: overblow or overdraw (hole 5 here)
!Rest, breath
rChromatic harmonica: the bar means “slide pressed”

Frequently asked questions

Is Harmo Tabs free?

Yes. The online version (PDF) and the Windows version (PDF and Excel) are free. On Android the app is free with ads; Harmo Tabs Pro, a one-time purchase, removes ads and the signature and adds Excel export and the 16-hole chromatic.

Which files can I convert?

MIDI files (.mid, .midi) and karaoke files (.kar). With karaoke files, rests follow the lyric lines.

Which harmonicas are supported?

The 10-hole diatonic harmonica (Richter tuning) in all 12 keys, and 12-hole or 16-hole chromatic harmonicas.

What if a note cannot be played?

It shows as a red “?”. Change the key or octave, or transpose the song: the app shows how hard each option is.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs entirely on your phone or computer.
